What This Site Covers

This edition focuses on readable walkthrough prose, stable navigation, bilingual browsing, and a growing layer of original editorial commentary around the game.

English is the default at the site root, and every page can switch directly to its Chinese counterpart under /zh/. The reference side now mixes core guides with five feature essays written to add more value than a raw archive mirror.
